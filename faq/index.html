Common Questions

How do I find my driver at the airport?

Your driver will meet you in the arrivals hall with a sign bearing your name. Look for us after you collect your baggage.

What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor all incoming flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, your driver will be there when you land — no extra charge.

How much does an airport transfer cost?

Pricing depends on your destination. Downtown Whitehorse transfers start from $45 per person. Contact us for a quote to other locations.

Can I book for today?

Last-minute bookings are often possible. Contact us for availability. Advance booking is recommended during peak summer and winter seasons.

Do you serve areas outside Whitehorse?

Yes. We provide transfers to Haines Junction, Carcross, Tagish, Marsh Lake, and other Yukon communities by arrangement.

What vehicles do you use?

We use clean, well-maintained minivans and SUVs with luggage space. All vehicles are equipped for Yukon winter conditions.
