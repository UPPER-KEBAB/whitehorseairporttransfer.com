Will someone track my flight?＋
Ask the provider explicitly. A flight number alone does not confirm that monitoring or a delayed pickup is included.
What should a transfer quote include?＋
Confirm the complete route, taxes, luggage allowance, waiting time and any extra stops in writing.
Can I reserve a ride here?＋
